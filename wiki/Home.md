Alice is a local AI assistant that floats on the [niri](https://github.com/YaLTeR/niri) Wayland desktop. She lives in a storybook: idle as a slim spine on your screen, and opening up to read, talk and act when you call her. Everything runs on your own machine by default.

## Pages

- [[Installation]]: requirements, installing and uninstalling
- [[Usage]]: opening her, talking, typing and keybinds
- [[Features]]: everything she can do
- [[Configuration]]: settings, API keys and servers
- [[Privacy and Safety]]: what stays on your computer and what she asks before doing
- [[Phone Companion]]: the Android app
- [[Artwork]]: her pictures, and painting your own with a local ComfyUI
- [[Troubleshooting]]
- [[Development]]: the code layout, evals and tests

## Made with AI

Alice was developed with the help of AI coding assistants. The code is reviewed and tested on a real desktop, but expect rough edges, and read what an action will do before you approve it.
